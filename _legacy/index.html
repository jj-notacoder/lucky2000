About

Hit the jackpot, after dark.

Lucky 2000 is a midnight doughnut drop in Abu Dhabi — golden Italian-zeppole-meets-zalabiyeh, fried fresh and filled with playful, high-quality flavours. Mina style. Strong black sweet tea on the side. Every visit should feel like pulling the lever and watching the reels land.

Location

Down the block. Abu Dhabi.

Open Fridays, Saturdays & Sundays, 7 PM onwards. Follow the weekly drop on Instagram to see what’s starring this week.
